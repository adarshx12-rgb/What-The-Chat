Why What The Chat exists

Chat stories depend on timing: a reply that arrives late, a typing indicator that lingers, or a message that disappears. What The Chat is a free creative tool for staging those moments and turning them into screenshots and vertical videos.

It is built for writers, video creators, educators, and anyone who needs a fictional chat for a story, demonstration, or storyboard.

From script to screen

Write both sides of a conversation, choose Android or iOS styling, and customize the contact name, avatar, wallpaper, and bubble colors. Reveal messages manually or replay a saved take with your recorded timing.

Export the preview as a 1080 × 1920 PNG or record a 9:16 video directly from the chat canvas. Because the preview itself is recorded, the mouse cursor and the rest of your screen stay outside the video.

Your workspace, in your browser

You can use the studio without an account. Projects and imported images are saved in your browser on your device, with JSON import and export for backups. The optional MP4 converter runs on your own computer.

Browser storage is not a cloud backup. Export projects you want to keep before clearing site data or changing devices. Read our privacy policy for details about local storage and external services used by the website.
